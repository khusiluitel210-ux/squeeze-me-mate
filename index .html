Two sides of the glass

Washing the outside and glazing the inside are different jobs, so we treat them as two separate services. Book one, or book both for the full inside-and-out clean.

Outside

External wash

The outside of your glass cops the dust, salt spray, pollen and bird mess. We scrub it back, then squeegee it dry so it stays streak-free in the Queensland sun.

  • Outside glass washed and squeegeed
  • Frames and sills wiped down
  • Fly screens dusted off
  • Cobwebs cleared from around the window
Inside

Internal glaze

Inside glass is detailed by hand and polished until there’s nothing left but the view. Towels go down first, so your floors and furniture stay dry.

  • Inside glass polished streak-free
  • Fingerprints, smudges and pet nose prints gone
  • Inside sills wiped
  • No drips on floors or furniture

Want both? Choose “External wash & internal glaze” on the quote form and we’ll price the full job together.

No middlemen. Just us and a squeegee.

A lot of what people pay for window cleaning never reaches the person doing the cleaning. Franchise fees, call centres and booking platforms each take a slice, and that slice ends up on your quote.

Squeeze Me Mate works differently. We’re direct to customer: when you call or send a quote request, you’re talking to the people who’ll turn up with the buckets. There’s no franchise, agency or app between us and you. That keeps our costs down, and we pass the savings straight on to you.

Lower prices don’t mean cutting corners. Every job gets the same care, from the corners of the glass to the gunk in the tracks. If something isn’t right, tell us and we’ll make it right.
